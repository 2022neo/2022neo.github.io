Yuwen Ji

Joint PhD at Zhejiang University and Westlake University. My research focuses on multimodal learning, vision-language-action models, human motion generation.

Research

I am interested in building machine learning systems that connect language, vision, motion, and structured reasoning. My recent work includes in-context learning, graph neural network explainability, geo-entity resolution, and scholar profiling.

  • Multimodal learning and embodied intelligence
  • Human motion generation and vision-language-action modeling
  • Language, graph, and vision models for structured reasoning
